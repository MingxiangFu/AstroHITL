# Floating preview and labels that stay inside the board

> **中文说明**：UMAP 预览改为不改变布局的浮层，类别图细边和左侧竖条使用类别色，英文按钮完整留在各自区域内。

## Preview and category color

The UMAP sample card is absolutely positioned, so showing or hiding it does not shift the plots. Preview images on a category card use a thin border in that category's tag color. The card's left bar uses the same color.

> **中文说明**：预览层浮在图上；卡片上的图像细边和左侧竖条都用该类别的标签色。

## Labels

Buttons for a classification class sit on a row under the class name, including a long name. English labels on the mode buttons, recommend tools, and training buttons are shorter, and those controls wrap inside the column instead of drawing past its edge.

> **中文说明**：分类类的按钮换到名称下方；英文模式按钮缩短并在栏内换行。
